Authentic 22 Days 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training Program in Pokhara Nepal

Pokhara Yoga School and Retreat Center offers the best 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Pokhara Nepal in the lush greenery of the Himalayan forest. We welcome learners from all over the world aspiring to be certified Yoga teachers. Completing our 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course (YTTC 200 Hour) will make you eligible for the Yoga Alliance 200 RYT Accreditation, letting you become a Registered Yoga Teacher (RYT). You can teach Yoga in any school, college, professional sports institute, or Yoga Learning Centre in any part of the globe.

Learn from our certified and highly experienced Yoga Teachers Trainers and Spiritual Masters. Set for a soul-enriching and life-transformative journey with a profound understanding of Yoga from learning core basics and fundamental principles to various Asanas, and philosophical aspects to mastering Hatha Yoga and Ashtanga Yoga.

Goals of Our 200-Hour YTT Program

As a curious and committed learner, you must know the goals of our 200-Hour YTTC program.

  1. Get a practical, comprehensive, hands-on training Yoga program regardless of your knowledge and practice level
  2. Impart in-depth knowledge of yoga asanas, chakras, human anatomy, Ayurveda and meditation.
  3. Make you a Registered Yoga Teacher (RYT) providing you a certificate after completion of YTT 200 Hours
  4. Enhance your confidence and empower you to transform your life through profound knowledge, the right beliefs and the right actions.
  5. Get deeply interested and committed to advanced learning of Yoga and teach to enrich the lives of others

Who Are Eligible to Join Our 200-Hour YTT Program?

Our 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Nepal program is suitable for all Yoga learners who are above 18 years old and less than 60 years of age. The learners must be free from any health issues.

Besides these, there is no other specific eligibility condition. Learners from any nationality, sex, religion, or background are welcome to join our 200-Hour YTT program.

  1. Learn the Ancient Science of Yoga- the Spiritual Discipline based on Sanatan Yoga
  2. Be a Certified Yoga Instructor/Teacher with a globally recognized certification
  3. Delve deep into the Subtle Art and Science of Healthy Living
  4. Interacting with other Yoga Gurus and Yogis from all corners of the earth
  5. Transforming life with knowledge, mindfulness and practising a healthy lifestyle and diet.
  6. Contributing your best to the welfare of society by imparting Yoga knowledge and spreading positivity
  7. Enhanced self-confidence and self-control with meditation, mindfulness and advanced Yoga knowledge
Salient Features of Our 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training

We provide safe and comfortable accommodation in our large Yoga Retreats designed to give you a quiet nature getaway. Located far from the din and bustle of city life, nestled in the hills between farming land and hiking trails, it gives you the most delightful, natural, relaxed and peaceful ambience where you can unwind, meditate, connect yourself with nature and enjoy yoga classes, spacious accommodation and vegetarian meals.

  1. Introduction to yogic lifestyle and philosophy
  2. Learn to master Hatha and Ashtanga Yoga

How to Join Our 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training Nepal Program

Our 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training starts on the 07th of every month and ends on the 28th of every month and another batch or session starts from the 15th of every month and ends on the 05th of the next month, available till November 2025.

The Yoga training and Yoga retreat package includes Yoga classes, accommodation and vegetarian meals. To apply for training, you need to Register Here. You can book a retreat package and secure a spot by depositing USD 200 cost in advance.

As our Yoga Teacher Training program is available on a First Come First Served basis, you need to book your slot soon to ensure that you don’t miss anything.
