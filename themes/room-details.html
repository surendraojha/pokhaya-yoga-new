Rooms and Facilities

Our Private rooms come fitted with king sized double beds. Our mattresses have been selected to provide the highest comfort and quality. The rooms are spacious and come with attached bathrooms. The bathrooms included a shower, toilet and sink with 24/7 hot and cold water. The room is furnished with cupboards to hold personal items, a Tea table where you can find a kettle and items to make yourself a tea in the comfort of your own room. Each room is fitted with a medium sized mirror in the main space and also a small mirror in the bathroom. The rooms have a small attached balcony with views of surrounding nature areas.

Step out onto your private balcony to savor panoramic views of the sparkling ocean and breathtaking sunsets. With modern amenities and elegant decor, your stay promises to be an unforgettable experience of comfort and sophistication.
