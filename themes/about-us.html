Location

Infinity Resort is nestled remotely among the green hills overlooking Phewa lake, whilst being only a 15-minute walk from the heart of Pokhara. Pokhara is in the geographical centre of Nepal and its second largest city, after the capital Kathmandu. The lakeside of Pokhara is the tourist hub of the city, gathering travellers, aircraft pilots, paragliders and trekkers from all corners of the world.

How to reach us at Infinity Resort, Lakeside (Sedi Heights), Pokhara 33700, Nepal:

By air: The closest international airport is Tribhuvan Airport in Kathmandu, with multiple flights from/to various destinations every day. Pokhara has its own domestic airport, so you can get here without taking the lengthy bus trip from Kathmandu.

By land: If you are looking for a more budget-friendly option to get to Pokhara from Kathmandu, you can hop on a tourist bus that takes approximately 7-8 hours. Private car services are also available.

From India: There are multiple entry points on the border to Nepal, including: Kakkarbhitta (West Bengal), Biratnagar and Birgunj (Bihar), Sanauli, Nepalgunj, Dhangadi and Mahendranagar (Uttar Pradesh). From there, you can take a bus or car ride to Pokhara.

Our Vision
Mission:

Our mission is to create a welcoming and inclusive yoga school in Pokhara where people of all ages and backgrounds can experience the transformative power of yoga. As a leading yoga retreat center in Nepal, we are dedicated to promoting physical health, mental clarity, and emotional balance through mindful movement, conscious breathing, and meditation.

We believe yoga is more than physical exercise—it is a holistic way of life rooted in awareness, compassion, and self-discovery. Through expert instruction, personalized guidance, and a supportive learning environment, our goal is to help students develop strength, flexibility, mindfulness, and inner resilience—both on and off the mat.

Objectives:

We value our registered teachers, healers and local yoga studios and are always committed to helping them improve their knowledge and spiritual growth by giving them an opportunity to join our yoga events, workshops, and Teacher Training classes. Our objective is to provide a yogic friendly, healthy and safe atmosphere to our students that will empower them to overcome their limitations and insecurities. We are dedicated to each and every individual and are passionate in trying to maximize their potential through various yoga asana, mediation and pranayama workshops.

Beliefs:

We believe that the practice of yoga can directly enhance and increase the mental, physical, and spiritual health of all people who practice. We believe in creating a temple of learning, where knowledge flourishes and skills grow to their fullest potential. We believe that every individual is unique and can bring an own experience, knowledge, and personality to the courses. We value honest friendships and connections.
